# Sam Obila Allela

**Machine Learning Engineer | Robotics & Applied AI**

I work at the intersection of machine learning, robotics, and intelligent systems, with an interest in the computational foundations of learning, representation, and agency.

My engineering experience involves developing machine learning capabilities for assistive robotic systems, particularly speech intelligence, real-time inference, and human-robot interaction. At Zerobionic Africa, I contribute to robotic platforms deployed across 78 schools in Kenya.

Beyond applied engineering, my research interests concern the mechanisms through which artificial systems construct internal representations, adapt to environmental change, and acquire goal-directed behavior. I am particularly interested in predictive coding, active inference, dynamical systems, biologically inspired learning, and foundational questions in AI alignment.
